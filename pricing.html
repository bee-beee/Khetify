Frequently Asked Questions

How does the commission work?

On the free plan, we charge 2-3% commission per successful order. This covers platform maintenance, payment processing, and customer support. Pro farmers pay just ₹99/month (or ₹999/year) with minimal 0-0.5% commission.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes! You can upgrade to Pro anytime. If you're on Pro and want to downgrade, you can do so at the end of your billing cycle. No lock-in periods.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and digital wallets. All payments are processed securely through our payment partner.

How do I get paid for my sales?

Payments are settled to your bank account within 3-5 business days after successful delivery. You can track all payments in your farmer dashboard.

Is there a minimum sales requirement?

No minimum sales requirements on any plan. Sell at your own pace. The Pro plan is recommended if you're making ₹3,300+ in sales per month (break-even point).

Do you offer refunds?

Pro plan subscriptions are refundable within 7 days of purchase. After that, we don't offer refunds, but you can cancel anytime to prevent future charges.
